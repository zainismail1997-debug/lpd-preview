About us

Supplying and installing wall protection across Western Canada since 2007.

Who we are

Est. 2007

Liquid Diamond Products is the leading Western Canadian distributor of wall protection products. We supply and install for hospitals, schools, care homes, food plants and commercial buildings.

We pick every product for our climate and test adhesion before it goes on the wall. We distribute FRP across Canada and the US, and we work directly with the manufacturers on every project.

Safety

2025 COR Employer Award for Excellence in Safety

The Alberta Construction Safety Association named Liquid Diamond Products the 2025 winner of its COR Employer Award for Excellence in Safety, 1 to 19 employees. The award was presented at the 2025 ACSA Conference in Calgary.

We hold a Certificate of Recognition (COR), issued jointly by the ACSA and the Government of Alberta.
