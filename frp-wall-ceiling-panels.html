What Does FRP stand for?

FRP panels stands for Fiberglass Reinforced Polyester panels. Manufactured using high strength polyester resin combined with fiberglass reinforcement to the FRP liner panel. It offers superior quality and durability. The result is a panel with a resin rich surface than is tough, strong and easy to clean.
