Puckboard materials (HDPE)

High-density polyethylene sheet for high-abuse walls.

Puckboard, sometimes called arena board or hockey board, is a versatile, adaptive plastic material. Puckboard is tough, resilient, and will take a beating without cracking or breaking.

PuckBoard plastic sheet is a superior alternative to wood or steel. It is commonly used for wall liners in moist, abusive areas because it is easy to keep clean; nothing sticks to it.

It is great for both indoor and outdoor applications. It can be formed into a radius and will cut easily with standard tools. Best of all, it has an high impact strength, it is easy to clean, and is non-fading.

Applications

Puckboard can be used for the following applications:

  • Hockey Arenas - for both indoor and outdoor facilities
  • Sport Facilities - can be used for sports arenas for soccer, squash, etc.
  • Animal housing - cattle, horse, hog, goats, sheep, rabbits, fish, dogs, cats, etc.
  • Wash Bays and other areaws that require and easy to clean surface
  • Ice huts, structures, wall protection liners, etc.
  • Automotive raceways
  • Chemical transfer
  • Liners
  • Wall Covers
  • Slides and snowboarding ramps
  • Sled runners and mud deflectors
  • Anything in general where you need an easy to clean surface

Easy to Cut

It can be cut with a regular saw and shaved, chiseled or drilled to fit your exacting standards. It is slippery and snowboards slide very well on the surface.
